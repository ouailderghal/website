### Towards a Constraint-Driven Deployment Process for Cloud-Native Applications

**Ouail Derghal, Jean-Christophe Bach, Fabien Dagnat**

*2025 IEEE International Conference on Cloud Engineering (IC2E)*, pp. 85–93, IEEE, 2025.<br>
🏆 Runner-up, Best Paper Award

[PDF](https://hal.science/hal-05206415v1/file/IC2E2025-author_version-camera_ready.pdf) · [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11196231/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&user=pc6192kAAAAJ&citation_for_view=pc6192kAAAAJ:u5HHmVD_uO8C)

We address deployment fragmentation in cloud environments by proposing a
constraint-based approach to deployment, where constraints are declarative
and treated as first-class inputs, tackling technological lock-in,
multi-cloud complexity, and unclear role definitions among development
teams.
